2026 projection comparison

Frozen v1.43 versus Steamer dated March 25, 2026, and registered year-end 2026 actuals. The Steamer snapshot date is supplied by the user. This is historical reporting. No model or forecast values were changed.

Findings

Steamer has lower mean absolute error on all 39 scored statistics in the broad matched samples. Our rates are closer to Steamer’s accuracy than our workload estimates are. The gap is especially large for projected innings. This is a descriptive result for 2026, with no significance claim or model selection.

There are 929 matched hitter forecasts and 1,308 matched pitcher forecasts. Among players with positive actual exposure, 550 hitters and 660 pitchers have both forecasts. Batting rate comparisons use 549 players because one has no positive actual AB. Innings scoring includes another 512 matched pitchers with certified zero participation, for 1,172 total. Neither nonappearance event totals nor missing hitter participation are invented.

Playing time and skill

PA and IP measure opportunity errors. AVG, OPS, ERA and WHIP describe rate performance. HR per 600 PA and pitching SO/BB/HR per nine innings offer additional workload-neutral comparisons. Count-error decomposition holds actual workload fixed for the rate portion and attributes the remaining difference to workload at the projected rate. It is a diagnostic, not an achievable preseason forecast. Do not add absolute component errors: they can offset.

Scope and limitations

Exact MLBAM IDs are matched separately for hitters and pitchers. No name matching. One row in each Steamer file has no valid MLBAM ID and is excluded with an exception record. Source-published Steamer rates and decimal projected innings are preserved. Actuals use existing eligible labels and the certified pitcher G/OUTS participation supplement. Confirmed zero pitching contributes to IP/G scoring, not invented zero event statistics. Missing hitter appearances or missing event labels remain unknown. Rate errors require positive actual AB/innings. Coverage gaps are reported separately from matched accuracy.

v1.43 is a frozen historical reconstruction using 2022–2025 player history and priors through 2025. This report does not establish that our forecasts were actually published before the season. The 2027 minor-history challenger is not included. The 2026 outcomes were already reviewed and cannot serve as a fresh model-selection test. Differences in roster knowledge and playing-time assumptions can explain part of the comparison. No adoption decision or international translation is made here.

Files

matched_accuracy_summary.csv contains the full accuracy results. projection_agreement_summary.csv measures forecast similarity. player_metric_comparison.csv has all available forecast/actual values, including unmatched records. matched_actual_comparisons.csv contains the identical-player accuracy pool. coverage_summary.csv and player_coverage.csv expose missing players. Largest-difference/error files rank players separately by metric. count_error_decomposition.csv separates workload and rate effects. Source manifests and validation results preserve provenance.
