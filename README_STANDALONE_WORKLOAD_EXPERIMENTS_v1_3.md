## Two separate questions, not an ensemble

1. Does categorical role probability improve workload forecasting while retaining prior-role classification labels?
2. Can a prior-information growth/decline model improve PA/OUTS forecasting?

There is no combined winning-model release or automatic production promotion. Both selected experiments are reported separately against frozen v1.2. The raw candidates remain visible even if rejected. All published selected projections retain the exact frozen saves values. The probability experiment leaves hitters unchanged. The separate change experiment may alter hitter PA/counts only when its historical gate passes.

### Experiment 1: probability input ablation

The build reuses BOTH frozen chronological probability streams from v1.2: prior_role and categorical. It does not refit or retune the categorical classifier. Every row's latest role-fitting year must be earlier than that row's target year. 2026 source role records must still say NOT_READ for actual role. Duplicate, incomplete, nonnormalized or future-derived streams fail.

Two otherwise identical workload pipelines are fitted with the unchanged v1.2 expert settings:
- prior_probability_control: experts fit and mixed using prior-role probabilities
- categorical_probability_candidate: experts fit and mixed using categorical probabilities

Both use exactly the same eligible historical rows, target definitions, role cohorts, fallback rules and ridge settings. The probability stream is the only input difference. Workload experts' actual roles are training labels/cohorts; each row's probability features were generated by an earlier-season role fit, never by a classifier trained on that row's target outcomes.

Hard classification labels stay the frozen v1.2 prior_role labels for EVERY experiment. If the prior role was unknown, the existing fold-population fallback label is retained and the prior_role field remains UNKNOWN. No class threshold, minority-class acceptance criterion or old classification gate is relaxed. Categorical probabilities may be useful as workload inputs even when their argmax classes failed the old classification gate; the new experiment tests this separately.

The categorical workload candidate must pass historical workload gates against BOTH the prior-probability control AND frozen v1.2. Each comparison requires two earlier qualifying non-2020 folds, at least 30 paired examples per fold, >=1% reduction in equal-fold mean MSE and no more than 1% MAE deterioration. A prior-STARTER guard cohort (at least 20 per fold) additionally requires non-worse mean MSE and no more than 1% MAE deterioration. If either comparator or either cohort gate fails, selected_probability_experiment retains frozen v1.2.

### Experiment 2: direction plus magnitude of workload change

Separate hitter and pitcher models use only prior actuals, historical physical measurements, age/history/era fields and prior volume summaries. They do NOT use the new categorical probability stream, keeping the two questions distinct.

Anchor = strictly previous-year PA or OUTS. Prior source-2020 volume is normalized by fixed 162/60. Missing prior-year exposure stays unknown; there is no silent older-year anchor substitution. Known zero exposure remains zero.

On historical rows with an eligible target exposure, positive recorded G and known anchor:
- Growth label: target exposure > anchor.
- Non-growth label: target exposure <= anchor, including exact ties.
- A regularized binary logistic model estimates growth probability.
- Separate ridge models estimate positive growth magnitude and loss magnitude conditional on their respective labels. Zero change is a zero LOSS example.

Conditional forecast = anchor + P(growth) × expected gain - (1-P(growth)) × expected loss. Predicted loss is bounded by the anchor, gain by remaining headroom under fixed volume caps (800 PA / 900 OUTS), and final exposure is bounded to [0,cap]. Raw and bounded magnitude forecasts are retained for audit. These bounds are fixed controls, not fitted to evaluation results.

Direction penalty 0.05, at most 200 deterministic gradient iterations; magnitude ridge/preprocessing uses the existing fixed baseline controls. If direction class support is insufficient, the probability falls back to the fitting-fold growth frequency with add-one smoothing. If an anchor or either magnitude population is unavailable, retain the frozen forecast rather than fabricate a zero change. Inherited participation probability converts conditional exposure to expected exposure; no new absence-to-zero inference is made.

Selection is independent for hitters and pitchers. The candidate must pass the same historical MSE/MAE gate against frozen v1.2, plus a preseason-known guard: pitchers with prior STARTER role; hitters with normalized prior anchor >=300 PA. Failed/insufficient gates retain frozen v1.2. Growth classification accuracy does not by itself justify changing workload forecasts.

### Chronology and preprocessing

Historical report origins are 2019–2025. All fits use strictly earlier target years; each origin's decisions use only earlier out-of-sample validation results. Role probability inputs for workload training rows retain their own historical origin, not a re-estimate from the full outer training set. Training medians, scaling, missingness indicators and dropped columns are fit within each fold/model only.

Target 2020 is excluded from fitting/selection but retained in stress-test reporting. Source-2020 normalization is fixed. The builder reads eligible training labels only and feature-only 2026 rows. It does not parse holdout_matrix.parquet or holdout_labels.parquet; integrity checks may hash their bytes. Final models and selected 2026 projections freeze before the separate evaluator reads 2026 outcomes.

Actual role/exposure/growth cohorts are retrospective diagnostics, not available-at-draft selectors. The explicit direction target compares with the normalized prior anchor; the general actual-change cohort in workload scores compares observed raw prior volume, so source-2020 cohorts require that distinction.

## Forecast methods and files

workload_predictions.csv contains six methods in PA or OUTS units:
- frozen_v1_2
- prior_probability_control
- categorical_probability_candidate
- workload_change_candidate
- selected_probability_experiment
- selected_change_experiment

Common-pool scoring requires all six predictions and an eligible actual for a player/metric. Missing actuals stay unknown. Candidate fallbacks are real forecast behavior and remain in these pools; support flags show where models could apply. This avoids comparing a candidate's easy supported subset against the full baseline pool. Raw candidates are research diagnostics and are not the selected forecast policy.

Full count-component forecasts are published only for frozen_v1_2 and the two selected experiments. Counts scale with accepted PA/OUTS while retaining existing component-derived AVG/OBP/SLG/OPS/ERA/WHIP; zero new denominators produce null rates. Original zero exposure cannot supply missing skill components, so those cases retain the frozen forecast. Saves stay exact for all published selected forecasts. No joint team-level or full roster/coherence model is claimed. Full projections are streamed to limit memory use.

Build folder:
- workload_experiments_build_report.json: final independent choices, preservation and disclosure.
- historical_gate_evidence.json and rolling_choices.json: comparator/cohort evidence for every origin.

## Remaining limits

2026 has already been reviewed. It remains disclosed evaluation data, NOT a fresh untouched holdout. The defaults and experiments are research controls; no 2026 outcome is used for fitting or selection. Repeated historical model searches can overfit and require a future prospective freeze for independent evidence.

All appearance, injury, prospect, preseason roster-universe and retrospective-vintage limitations remain. Missing actuals are not nonappearance labels. Probability estimates are not guaranteed calibrated. Conditional models are evaluated on available eligible outcomes, not a verified full non-return population. No significance, production-readiness or draft-improvement claim is made. Synthetic tests do not establish forecast accuracy: full-data results must be run on your PC.
