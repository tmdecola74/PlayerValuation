STRENGTHENED STATCAST VALIDATION FOR THE 2027 PROJECTIONS

Decision: retain the working v1.43 baseline. The source-history and 2026 scope limitations have now been addressed for the specified GB/LD/FB family with documented exclusions. The unchanged model still fails the required minimum improvement. No 2027 production forecast or DRAFTTOOL setup was replaced.

1. Older archives and compatible feature definition
Existing local files supplied every needed archive year; no new statistics were downloaded. We verified 1,471 daily windows across 2021-2026, including raw CSV, normalized parquet and daily schedule hashes. Each season's pitch identities, date/row totals and regular-season game coverage were checked. Cached full-season MLB schedules were bound to the existing certified coverage reports; team game counts matched cached standings. Cancelled or postponed entries marked abstract Final were excluded with the original completed-game predicate, not mistaken for missing played games.

The source was not a launch-angle approximation. We counted actual Statcast bb_type categories ground_ball, line_drive and fly_ball on type=X in-play records, divided by ALL in-play BBE. We required at least 30 BBE and no unclassified in-play records. Popups were counted separately to audit complete classification, not added as a model predictor. MLB's documentation identifies these pitch-result and category fields: https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs . No spray-direction, angle bin or tracking-position proxy replaced the feature definition.

Before admitting older reconstructed observations, the same count/denominator calculation had to match every previously admitted 2023-2025 research bundle. All 3,593 matched, with no denominator/category-rate disagreement under the existing 1e-8 tolerance. This verifies the deterministic aggregate feature bridge; it is not an independent certification of each unpublished leaderboard event-membership list. Archived physical pitches retain unique identities and complete scheduled-game coverage.

The bridge admitted 2,429 additional player-season bundles: 2021 hitters 546/pitchers 676 and 2022 hitters 554/pitchers 653. They are explicitly labeled archive reconstructions, not newly obtained official leaderboard exports. Smaller samples and incomplete classifications remain excluded. Original snapshots and prior feature tables are unchanged.

2. Full 2026 export scope check
The 2026 archive contains 716,792 pitches and 124,611 in-play BBE across 2,429 completed regular-season games. The 241 contiguous daily windows through October 27 cover every completed regular-season game in the cached full-season schedule and match standings totals; the window end date alone was not used to assert completeness.

We checked all 1,495 hitter/pitcher export rows. Of these, 1,493 exactly match the categorical archive rates and BBE denominator. Applying the fixed 30-BBE threshold leaves 1,193 eligible row bundles. A total of 302 are withheld: 300 are below the sample threshold and two have an unresolved classification difference. Anthony Seigler (MLBAM 678011) and Lake Bachar (669199) have one unclassified archive in-play record in their respective player totals, with a line-drive-rate disagreement. Their denominators match but that does not resolve the classification. We did not fill in a category, replace an export value or infer which vintage is correct. The physical event trace is provided.

No novel minimum was tuned from forecast errors. Existing sample/definition masks remain in force, and only rows passing both the previous eligibility and the new scope audit enter the rerun. Source rows absent from an export are inventoried separately. The result is validated aggregate input scope for this three-feature experiment with explicit exclusions, not blanket approval of EV, bat tracking, direction or other Statcast families.

3. Unchanged challenger and three forecast folds
Predictors remain GB/LD/FB fractions, prior-four-season BBE times 1:2:3:4 pooling, target component-rate residuals against the v1.43-style baseline, ridge penalty 20, training-only standardization, z clipping to +/-4, target-exposure weights capped at 600, minimum 100 cases, half-strength residuals and +/-50% count caps. Hitter targets remain 1B/2B/3B; pitcher targets remain SO/BB. PA/IP and all previously frozen opportunity/skill anchors remain unchanged.

The previous baseline, feature-pooling, case-construction and application function texts have identical hashes. The fit function's only executable edit extends its allowed earlier target years from 2024-2025 to 2022-2025. Historical scoring now has supported 2023,2024,2025 origins. Each model fits only on targets earlier than its forecast year. The exact original adoption-guard function is reused with documented method aliases. Thresholds, recent-year checks, component count checks and low-history checks were not relaxed.

The model is no longer blocked by insufficient folds. Across the three supported development folds, the hitter mean objective MSE ratio is 0.992680, or a 0.732% improvement; the pitcher ratio is 0.997326, or a 0.267% improvement. Both fall short of the required 1% improvement. This is the only remaining guard failure for either role. These are equal-fold objective MSE ratios, not changes in MAE/RMSE or independent accuracy percentages. The historical seasons were previously reviewed; this is not a fresh untouched holdout or a significance claim.

4. Freeze and the 2027 forecasts
After the historical gate decisions, final research models fit 2022-2025 targets and freeze. Only then were 2026 FanGraphs labels opened, solely as 2027 player history. No 2026 target residual or forecast error was fit, scored, used for selection or used to adjust a threshold. The 2021/2022 Statcast observations expand historical calibration examples; they do not enter an individual 2027 player's 2023-2026 feature window.

All 2,511 working forecasts were reproduced from the original2027 baseline. The guarded candidate equals the baseline for all 42,224 statistical fields because both roles failed adoption. Exploratory predictions remain available with coverage/fallback reasons, and no source gap is filled with minor or international coefficients. Workload, parks and international translations stay unchanged.

5. Conditional player review and next decision
The requested adoption review was conditional on a gate pass. Neither role passed, so no player-level overrides or adoption recommendation were made. We provide ranked exploratory differences for diagnosis, not as an adopted player list. Keep v1.43 as the working forecast. A broader Statcast predictor experiment would require its own specification and source-definition validation; it should not be created by weakening the 1% threshold or repeatedly tuning this recipe on the same results.
