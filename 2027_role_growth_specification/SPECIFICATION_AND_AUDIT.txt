2027 ROLE AND DEVELOPMENT MODEL: SPECIFICATION AND READINESS AUDIT
As of November 26, 2026

DECISION
Retain the 2027 v1.43 working forecast. Historical usage-based development can proceed to data-adapter and cohort construction, but a chart-conditioned model cannot yet be validated. Current depth charts support conditional 2027 scenarios after explicit workload assumptions are registered. No model was fitted and no forecast error was scored in this deliverable.

WHAT IS AVAILABLE
Annual eligible MLB labels span 2015–2025 for historical selection. The existing pitcher overlay supplies 4,230 certified zero-participation player-seasons in its 2019–2025 cohorts. Cached game boxscores are present for 2019–2026, with original season certificates and sampled fields for per-game pitching outs, starts and batting order. Daily Statcast source windows are registered for 2015–2025, and earlier work verified complete 2021–2026 pitch scope. These are raw/registered sources, not already validated new model inputs. 2026 is allowed only as 2027 history.

THE HISTORICAL-CHART GAP
The scoped filename search found no certified 2023/2024/2025 preseason role snapshots. Of 30 standalone team workbooks, 30 are byte-identical to the new ZIP and 0 differ without certified preseason capture dates. A 2026 filename, Excel creation date or acquisition-year column does not establish a valid historical forecast vintage. FanGraphs projected-stat exports are external benchmarks, not our independent role model or timestamped historical roster evidence.

NEW WORKLOAD DESIGN
Separate skill, role/opportunity and participation. Use a distribution over future roles rather than an average of old annual totals. Estimate appearance, conditional role transitions and joint games/outings and workload per appearance. A recent full-time season informs next year's role; debut-year totals inform uncertainty and capacity rather than mechanically averaging down a healthy assumed full-time role. Do not guarantee annual growth. Team opportunity and replacement/depth allocations must reconcile. The first candidate holds skill rates fixed; age/development is a later experiment.

HOW THIS DIFFERS FROM THE REJECTED EXPERIMENT
The previous structured pitcher model already separated appearance, role, games and innings per outing, and failed adoption. Repeating that decomposition alone is not a new evidentiary basis. Its coefficients remain inactive. This proposal first reconstructs actual recent role transitions and per-game starts/relief innings, then separately admits chart context only when historical vintages are available. No new performance claim is made before tests.

VALIDATION PROTOCOL
Prepare 2023, 2024 and 2025 forecast origins using strictly earlier training targets. Keep the original >=3 supported folds, >=30 main rows/fold, >=1% mean objective MSE improvement, <=2% metric/recent/low-history degradation and cohort support requirements; retain applicable role/freshness and complete-count safeguards. Use identical supported memberships, report unknown outcomes and fallbacks, and include certified nonappearances. No 2026 forecast errors for fitting, tuning, selecting features or choosing thresholds. Historical errors already reviewed are development evidence, not a new independent holdout. No automatic adoption.

THE CUTOFF MATTERS
The current chart snapshot is November 26, 2026. A faithful historical version requires evidence available by November 26 before each forecast season. March preseason snapshots support a separately labeled spring forecast task, not a retrospective November forecast. Source retrieval date and effective transaction date are different fields. Retrospective stat revisions and schedule/calendar context must be documented. Never use target-year roles, injuries, actual innings or current service time as historical predictors.

READINESS LIMITS
Hitter annual G/PA is available, but a hitter-wide nonappearance certificate was not found. Positive-only training would omit demoted/inactive players and is insufficient for unconditional workload adoption. Cached boxscore availability is promising; schema samples are not a full parsing/reconciliation audit. Pitch data alone misses some no-pitch appearances and does not provide medical absence causes. Annual mixed-role IP/GS is not innings per start. Raw age coverage must be audited for meaning and dates; current chart AGE/service cannot be backdated. Missing IL evidence does not imply health. Missing chart players do not receive zero workload.

WHICH 2027 OUTPUTS CAN BE PUBLISHED
There are 772 primary-roster entries with same-role baseline skill support suitable for conditional scenario construction, and 8 without a baseline skill line. Publish chart-listed roles and baseline-versus-assumed-volume sensitivity with assumptions, timestamps and unchanged rate provenance. A scenario may state 'if this everyday/rotation role and this availability/workload occur'; do not attach invented scenario probabilities, expected-workload claims or calibrated intervals. Scenario counts are not adopted forecasts and are not fed into DRAFTTOOL. R/RBI/W/QS/SV/HLD need explicit context rules or remain unavailable. The Stewart and Misiorowski templates intentionally leave workload numbers unset.

BUILD ORDER
1. Reconcile historical game-by-game usage and start/relief labels. 2. Certify historical hitter appearance/nonappearance. 3. Freeze a fully executable usage-transition estimator, support minima and selection contract before scoring. 4. Test usage-only workload against v1.43 through 2025. 5. Register and publish chart-conditioned 2027 scenarios separately. 6. Acquire archived chart/availability data for chart-dependent historical validation. 7. Audit DOB/age/experience and separately specify player-development adjustments. 8. Review complete player changes, coverage, team totals and fallback behavior before adoption.

ADDITIONAL ARCHIVES REQUESTED
Highest priority: all-team role snapshots for historical origins with source capture dates and stable IDs. Next: longitudinal dated IL/roster transactions and a complete DOB/debut register. No 2027 FanGraphs projected-stat export is required to build our own model; any downloaded external forecast must remain a named comparison or external-opportunity arm.
