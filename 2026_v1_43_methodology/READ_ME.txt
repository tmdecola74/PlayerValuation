2026 v1.43 projection methodology

Read 2026_v1_43_projection_methodology.pdf or the browser HTML version. Both contain the same ten sections.
Key finding: final v1.43 uses eligible 2022-2025 MLB FanGraphs count history plus component regression toward eligible MLB population rates. Statcast physical metrics, MiLB, NPB/KBO and park factors are not active predictors in that release. Statcast research and audit roles are documented separately.

The complete saved 2026 release was replayed exactly: 2,564 player-role forecasts, 43,128 field comparisons, all nine population priors. No forecasts or source files were changed.
statistic_source_map.csv: each of the 34 projected statistical fields and its actual calculation path.
component_calculation_trace.csv: per-player component numerators, exposure normalization, priors and regression.
worked_examples.json and worked_example_source_seasons.csv: Judge, Skenes, Webb and Gausman examples.
source_manifest.json and the input inventory/mask/overlay CSVs: exact file provenance and eligibility scope.
The three-year rebuild and later workload/minor/international experiments are separate and are not represented as v1.43.
