Why the workload challenger failed

Diagnostic only. No model fitted, forecast changed or adoption guard relaxed.

Principal findings

PRINCIPAL FINDINGS

The failed challenger did not retain each player's numeric recent workload. It assigned a shared workload to everyone in a categorical role pool. In2024, 289 established regular hitters with prior workloads from 70 to 753 PA all received 393.2 PA. The source code and all8,134 supported traces confirm this behavior. None used the global pool: the loss of detail occurred even in the full latest/recent/experience pools.

Among recently active hitters, median probability shrinkage toward a parent was5.8%; among pitchers8.3%. These weights alone do not explain the failure. The absolute conditional workloads were peer averages with no individual PA/IP anchor. Parent conditional-mean borrowing is traced separately; its share is arithmetic, not a causal attribution of error. Large prior workloads can be pulled down while small prior workloads are pushed up. Workload-band tables show both directions; aggregate bias alone hides this compression.

For recently active established/stable hitters, mean fold MSE worsened44.39%; the recent-callup proxy was nearly unchanged (+0.04%), and the role-change proxy worsened15.09%. Recently active pitcher groups worsened2.40%,12.65% and10.67%, respectively. These are descriptive partitions/proxies with sample sizes, not new adoption tests. Returners are reported separately outside the recent-positive subset.

The known-zero hitter guard has a definition mismatch, not an absence of all supporting evidence. Its original freshness label has zero explicit-zero rows in each scoring fold. The separate pre-origin certificate supplies 522, 527 and 597 supported prior-zero-PA cases. All have missing original PA, so unchanged freshness calls them unknown. A later preregistered protocol can align the cohort ledger without changing baseline numbers or15-row/2% thresholds. That is not a retroactive pass: the active-player accuracy failure remains.

Recommend exactly one next experiment: learn workload change around each player's own latest reconciled workload instead of replacing it with an absolute group mean. Retain certified nonappearances as negative-change outcomes and do not add a universal growth bonus. Register the joint-vector/feasibility, shared cohort ledger, support and fallback rules before a separate test. This recommendation is informed by already reviewed development errors and is not fresh independent validation. No such model was built or tested here.

Stewart and Misiorowski remain unscored source-history illustrations. Their2025 debuts kept them outside these prior-MLB-history scoring folds. No2027 challenger projection was manufactured and no2026 projection error was calculated.

DEFINITIONS
Error is forecast minus actual; negative bias means underprojection. Fold-mean MSE ratios reproduce the original convention. Summed error contributions and pooled counts are separately labeled. Recent-call-up is a proxy for fewer than two positive MLB seasons in the prior four-year window, not independently verified rookie/service status. Role-change uses only pre-origin annual-to-annual or annual-to-last60 state changes. Returner is the existing observable earlier-substantial/no-recent-positive cohort, not a future signing. Primary diagnostic groups are mutually exclusive in returner/callup/role-change/established/other order; overlap tables are explicitly labeled. These groups do not alter scoring or adoption.

POOLING MECHANISM
The executable uses categorical latest/recent/experience groups, not each player's numeric latest workload. Every player in the same selected pool receives the same workload. Full-specific pools still discard within-group individual variation. Backoff can occur because a pool lacks200 rows or20 zero/positive outcomes. The conditional means additionally inherit their parent in states with fewer than20 cases, or shrink toward the parent with50 pseudo-rows. Probability smoothing and workload-mean smoothing are separate; node-level arithmetic traces do not constitute a new model or forecast-error test.

SUBGROUP SUPPORT
The failed guard uses unchanged baseline freshness. Original missing PA is UNKNOWN_RECENT, even when the candidate's separately certified source ledger establishes zero PA. The support crosswalk distinguishes data absence from a definition/mapping mismatch. Existing numerical thresholds and failed decisions are not changed. A future protocol must register consistent as-of zero-versus-missing cohort definitions for both arms, document full-cohort IDs and secondary-role fallbacks, and still fail unsupported guards. Sufficient certified counts do not establish acceptable accuracy and are not a retroactive pass.

ILLUSTRATIVE CASES
Stewart and Misiorowski are source-history illustrations only. Their2025 MLB debuts leave them outside the prior-MLB-history scoring pool for2023–2025. No2027 candidate projection is manufactured for this diagnostic, and2026 performance is not used as a forecast-error target. Their histories cannot determine a replacement formula.
